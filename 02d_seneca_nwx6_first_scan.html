About this graphic

Crops of GHD’s figures from each quarterly report. The yellow shading is added by Full Circle Future: it marks the Stage 6 grid squares that appear nowhere in GHD’s Q2 2023 monitoring tables, matched square by square to the grid numbers printed on each map, and repeated on the Q3 2023 map to show the same ground. The two figures are drawn at different scales, so match locations by the grid numbers printed in each square.

Stage 6 was already holding waste by 2019, when the operator planned new gas wells there. Two of the 13 Stage 6 exceedances (grids 1310 and 1311) lie just east of the area shown.

Sources: GHD, Second Quarter Surface Monitoring at Seneca Meadows Landfill (July 25, 2023), Figure 3, Western Expansion Grid Locations, and Tables 1–6; GHD, Third Quarter Surface Monitoring at Seneca Meadows Landfill (Jan. 11, 2024), Figure 5, Northwest Expansion Grid Locations, and pp. 1–4. Stage 6 waste: Seneca Meadows 2019 and 2020 Annual Reports, Section 6.0. Exhibit: Full Circle Future.
