Why does methane matter?
Methane is a highly potent greenhouse gas, with roughly 80 times the short-term warming impact of carbon dioxide. Large landfill methane releases can also indicate other harmful pollutants, including benzene, hydrogen sulfide, and PFAS. Proven, cost-effective solutions exist to significantly reduce these emissions.
