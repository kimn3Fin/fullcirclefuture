High Acres: methane seen from space, March 2026

Planet’s Tanager satellite detected a methane plume over High Acres on March 2, 2026, spreading several kilometres downwind from where it starts.

Methane plume: Carbon Mapper, from Planet’s Tanager satellite pass of March 2, 2026 (plume tan20260302t171105c63s4001-E). No emission rate was estimated; wind from the HRRR weather model. Plume colours are adapted from Carbon Mapper’s plume image (light = less methane, dark red = more).

A plume shows methane in the air at the moment of the pass, spreading downwind from where it starts; it does not by itself show exactly where on the ground a leak is.

Base map: Esri World Imagery; image dates vary. Exhibit: Full Circle Future.
