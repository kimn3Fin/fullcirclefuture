Ontario County Landfill: a methane plume seen from the air

An aircraft survey detected methane rising from the landfill on Oct. 5, 2022. Nothing in the federal rule required anyone to investigate it.

Methane plume: Carbon Mapper, source CH4_6A_1000m_-77.07913_42.85373, plume GAO20221005t162241p0000-A, detected Oct. 5, 2022 by the Global Airborne Observatory aircraft (Arizona State University). Carbon Mapper did not estimate an emission rate for this plume. Wind about 3.1 m/s from the HRRR weather model; no wind direction was reported.

Plume colours are adapted from Carbon Mapper’s plume image (light = less methane, dark red = more). A plume shows methane in the air at the moment of a pass, spreading downwind from where it starts. A single detection shows what was in the air on that day; it does not show how long the release lasted, and it does not by itself tie the plume to any one leak recorded in the operator’s surface monitoring.

Base map: Esri World Imagery; image dates vary. Exhibit: Full Circle Future.
