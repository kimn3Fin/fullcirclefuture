Immune suppression

Reduces vaccine effectiveness and weakens the body's ability to fight infection

PFAS doesn't break down in nature on any human timescale — it builds up in the body over years of low-level exposure. The EPA set its first enforceable limit in 2024 at just 4 parts per trillion, yet some states and scientists conclude there is no truly safe level of exposure.
