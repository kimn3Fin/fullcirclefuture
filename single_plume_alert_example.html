What can I do?
Landfill operators in CA are not required to respond to large plumes detected remotely. You can encourage policymakers to follow the lead of states like Colorado and California, which have already updated their landfill regulations to require landfill operators to respond to large, "super-emitter" methane leaks that manual surface monitoring often misses.

Why does methane matter?
On its own, methane is a highly potent greenhouse gas with about 80 times the planet-warming power of carbon dioxide in the short term. It also often indicates the presence of other health-harming pollutants, including benzene, toluene, hydrogen sulfide, and "forever chemicals" or PFAS. The good news is that there are practical, common-sense solutions to dramatically reduce methane and other pollution from landfills.
